📌 About the Project

I worked on this project to understand how data analysis can help hospitals make better decisions. Hospitals generate a large amount of data every day, including patient appointments, waiting times, billing transactions, insurance coverage, and outstanding payments.

The goal of this project was to explore this data, identify meaningful patterns, and understand what the numbers reveal about hospital operations and financial performance.

Using Python and its data analysis libraries, I explored hospital appointment and billing data and created visualizations to make the findings easier to understand.

📊 Key Findings

The following are the findings recorded in my notebook.

🏥 Appointment Analysis

📌 Appointment Volume: General Medicine recorded the highest appointment volume, with 4,288 appointments.

⏱️ Waiting Time: General Medicine was identified as having a relatively high average waiting time.

✅ Appointment Completion: Approximately two-thirds of appointments were completed successfully.

📅 Busiest Day: Tuesday recorded the highest appointment demand.

⚠️ Cancellations: General Medicine had a high number of cancellations, making it a useful area for further investigation.

💰 Billing Analysis

🏆 Highest Gross Billing: Emergency recorded approximately ₹14.35 million in gross billing revenue.

💳 Payment Status: Approximately 71% of billing records were marked as paid.

🛡️ Insurance Coverage: Bajaj Allianz recorded the highest total insurance coverage, at approximately ₹2.26 million.

📈 Revenue Trends: The analysis showed relatively stable billing revenue during 2024 and much of 2025, followed by a decline toward the end of 2025 and into 2026.

⚠️ Outstanding Amounts: Emergency had the highest outstanding amount at approximately ₹20.97 million, followed by Cardiology at ₹17.18 million and Orthopedics at ₹13.04 million.

Note: These figures reflect the findings recorded in the notebook. They should be validated against the underlying data before being used for actual business decisions.

💡 Business Insights

The analysis helped identify several areas that could benefit from further investigation.

🕒 Patient Experience: Investigate waiting times in departments with high appointment volumes.

📅 Appointment Management: Understand the reasons behind cancellations and no-shows.

👨‍⚕️ Resource Planning: Review doctor availability and staffing during busy periods.

📉 Revenue Monitoring: Investigate the decline in billing revenue observed toward the end of the analyzed period.

💳 Payment Follow-up: Review outstanding payments to identify opportunities to improve collection.

🛡️ Insurance Analysis: Compare insurance providers based on coverage, claim approvals, and payment processing.

These are potential areas for improvement identified through the analysis, not confirmed causes of the observed patterns.
